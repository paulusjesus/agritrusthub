About AgriTRUST

Agri for the land. TRUST for everything else.

We started AgriTRUST in Namibia because farmers here were losing money in ways that had nothing to do with farming. Buyers who never paid. Loans due before the rains. Photos of someone else's cattle used to sell cattle that didn't exist. We think a farmer deserves the same financial tools and market reach as any other business.

Founder's story

Farmers deserve more than uncertainty.

Agriculture feeds everyone, yet a lot of the people doing the farming are cut off from the money it makes. I kept meeting farmers who couldn't find an honest buyer, or who sold far below what their crop was worth. Others lost a whole season to pests, drought, bad storage or fake seed.

Some wanted to export and had no idea where to start. Many needed money before planting, and the banks wanted repayments every month, long before anything was ready to sell. Plenty had goats, manure, tools or labour to offer but no cash to trade with.

Then there was the land. I saw young farmers with the skills to grow food and nowhere to grow it, a few kilometres from fields that had been sitting empty for years.

And too often, farmers had to negotiate blind. They couldn't prove the quality of what they'd grown, so the buyer set the price. Many were also wary of apps that took their data and gave them nothing back.

We built AgriTRUST to change that.

On AgriTRUST a farmer can sell with payment held safely in escrow, borrow against the coming harvest, and swap goods when cash is short. They can lease land, check a grade before naming a price, and get help from a mentor. The farmer owns their records, and we don't sell them.

"Our job is to connect the soil to the market, and the landowner to the farmer, and to make sure the farmer is the one who gets paid."
